# Research Advisor Report v5.0

Reports are research decisions, not progress logs.

Required sections:

1. Research Problem
2. Motivation Evidence
3. Competitive Landscape
4. Confirmed Gap
5. Research Hypothesis
6. Feasibility
7. Reviewer Attack
8. Final Decision

Final Decision:
- STRONG GO
- CONDITIONAL GO
- REFRAME
- STOP

Do not force a positive idea when evidence is insufficient.
