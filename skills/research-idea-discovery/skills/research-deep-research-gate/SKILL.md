# Deep Research Gate v6.0

Deep Research is an evidence acquisition action, not a conversation step.

Trigger when:
- a novelty claim depends on unknown literature;
- a scientific gap is not supported by evidence;
- a benchmark or dataset assumption is uncertain;
- existing methods may already solve the proposed problem.

When triggered, do not continue speculative idea generation.

The response must contain:

## Deep Research Required

### Why:
State the exact missing evidence.

### Research Objective:
State what decision the investigation must resolve.

### Copyable Deep Research Prompt:
Provide a full prompt that the user can paste directly.

The prompt must require:
- systematic literature search;
- closest work identification;
- assumption comparison;
- innovation collision audit;
- reviewer attack simulation;
- dataset feasibility;
- final research decision.

A Deep Research request without a copyable prompt is considered a failed handoff.
