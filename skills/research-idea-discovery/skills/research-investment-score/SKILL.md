# Research Investment Score

Evaluate whether a research direction deserves months of investment.

Score:
- Scientific Importance /10
- Evidence Strength /10
- Novelty Confidence /10
- Feasibility /10
- Experimental Verifiability /10
- Resource Cost /10
- Publication Potential /10

Output:
HIGH INVESTMENT
MEDIUM INVESTMENT
LOW INVESTMENT
REJECT

The score is a decision aid, not a replacement for evidence.
A high score requires validated motivation and literature evidence.
