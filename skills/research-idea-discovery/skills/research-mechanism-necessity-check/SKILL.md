# Mechanism Necessity Check

Before accepting a proposed method, answer:

1. Why does this capability need to exist?
2. Why cannot simple baselines solve the problem?
3. Which assumption of previous methods is broken?
4. What scientific capability does the mechanism introduce?

Reject solutions that are only:
- adding attention;
- adding adapters;
- adding uncertainty modules;
- combining existing components without a new hypothesis.
