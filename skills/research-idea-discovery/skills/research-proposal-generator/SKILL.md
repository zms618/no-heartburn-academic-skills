# Research Proposal Generator v4.1

Generate a research proposal only after:
- motivation validation
- literature collision check
- mechanism necessity check
- feasibility audit

Never convert an unverified idea into a proposal.

Output:
1. Title
2. Problem statement
3. Scientific hypothesis
4. Evidence-backed motivation
5. Related work gap
6. Proposed mechanism
7. Experimental protocol
8. Expected contributions
9. Reviewer objections
10. Final recommendation
