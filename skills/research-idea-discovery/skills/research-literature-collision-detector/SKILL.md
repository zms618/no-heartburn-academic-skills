# Research Literature Collision Detector v4.1

Purpose:
Prevent unsupported novelty claims before research ideas are accepted.

Mandatory checks:
1. Search representative recent methods.
2. Identify whether the proposed problem definition already exists.
3. Identify whether the proposed mechanism is only a renamed existing technique.
4. Report collisions before accepting contributions.

Output:
- Existing related work
- Overlapping assumptions
- Remaining gap
- Novelty confidence

A research idea cannot receive GO status without collision analysis.
