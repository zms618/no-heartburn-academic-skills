# Deep Research Evidence Escalation & Return Bridge v6.0

Purpose:
Convert research uncertainty into a structured Deep Research handoff.

Rules:

1. If evidence is insufficient, generate a complete copyable research prompt.
2. Do not ask the user to design the investigation.
3. Do not expose internal workflow stages.
4. Do not require meaningless confirmations.

The handoff must preserve:
- research question;
- current hypothesis;
- known papers;
- rejected directions;
- missing evidence;
- feasibility constraints.

When a report returns:
- treat it as unverified evidence;
- extract claims and references;
- identify changed assumptions;
- update the research decision.

The goal is not producing more text.
The goal is reducing scientific uncertainty.
