# Research Execution Planner

## Purpose
Convert validated research directions into executable research plans.

## Rule
Do not plan implementation before problem validation.

## Output
Provide:
- 6 month roadmap
- minimum viable experiment
- baseline reproduction plan
- risk register
- fallback routes
- milestone criteria

## Research Execution Flow

Hypothesis validation
-> pilot experiment
-> method implementation
-> controlled evaluation
-> ablation
-> robustness analysis
-> paper preparation

## Failure Handling

If the pilot experiment does not support the hypothesis:
- revisit the research question
- do not blindly optimize the method
