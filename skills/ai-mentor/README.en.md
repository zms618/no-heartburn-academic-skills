# AI Mentor | A Research Problem-Solving Companion

**Bring your research questions to an AI mentor.** AI Mentor is a personal plugin for ChatGPT Web that helps you work through research ideas, literature, experiments, paper writing, scientific figures, submission preparation, and project progress. It uses your goals and available materials to clarify the problem, identify evidence that still needs checking, and suggest practical next steps.

It does not conduct research automatically or guarantee an answer to every question. Its purpose is to give you a structured discussion partner when you get stuck and help turn complex challenges into clear, assessable, actionable tasks.

> **Use it as a personal plugin in ChatGPT Web; it does not consume Codex-specific task quota.**

## What can you discuss with it?

- **Research directions and ideas:** clarify the research question, compare candidate directions, and examine assumptions and validation paths.
- **Literature and evidence:** organize related work, compare methods and findings, and distinguish verified evidence from questions that remain open.
- **Experiments and project progress:** discuss experiment design, baselines, ablations, failure analysis, planning, and progress reviews.
- **Writing and submission preparation:** shape a paper argument, improve its structure, plan figures, and inspect weaknesses from a reviewer perspective.
- **Ongoing research practice:** within the available conversation context and memory, keep track of decisions, unresolved questions, and milestones.

The package includes Supervisor Mode, Personal PhD Advisor, Idea Evaluator, Deep Research, Paper Writer, Figure Designer, Pre-submission Reviewer, and other Skills, alongside a research handbook and supporting references. Available capabilities depend on the models, files, and tools enabled in your ChatGPT account.

## Upload the ZIP and install it on ChatGPT Web

[Download the AI Mentor v1.4.0 plugin ZIP](../../downloads/ai-mentor-v1.4.0.zip)

1. Open ChatGPT's personal plugin management page from your profile or Settings → Plugins/Personal plugins, or visit [chatgpt.com/plugins?directoryTab=personal](https://chatgpt.com/plugins?directoryTab=personal).
2. Select **Add**, then **Upload plugin ZIP**.
3. Choose `ai-mentor-v1.4.0.zip`, wait for parsing, and follow the prompts to create or install it.
4. Confirm **AI Mentor** appears in your personal plugin list, then enable it from the tools/plugin picker in a new conversation.

Try starting with:

> I am working on [research area/project] and have run into this problem: [describe it]. First help me clarify the problem and identify what information or evidence is needed, then work with me to choose the most useful next step. Do not present assumptions as facts.

Button labels may vary by product version. A personal plugin is not thereby listed in the public plugin directory.
